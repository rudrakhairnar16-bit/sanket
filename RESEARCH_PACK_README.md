# SANKET 2.0 — Berserk Research Pack

This pack adds an original research and documentation layer to the SANKET prototype.

It includes:
- original conference-style paper draft;
- detailed research report;
- ISL literature review;
- technical recognition report;
- dataset/evaluation methodology;
- SRS/system design;
- ethics/privacy/accessibility framework;
- provenance/license matrix;
- evidence matrix;
- validation protocol;
- viva/judge research Q&A;
- references and BibTeX;
- OpenCode research integration task.

**Important:** Full copyrighted research papers are not redistributed here. The pack contains original summaries, synthesis, citations and project-specific analysis.
