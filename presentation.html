The Problem

18 million deaf citizens.
Fewer than 5% know ISL.

Every day, millions of deaf Indians walk into government offices and cannot communicate with the clerk across the counter.

18M
Deaf & hard-of-hearing citizens
3.5M
Government clerks serving municipal offices
<5%
Clerks with basic ISL proficiency

No accessible, workplace-integrated ISL training exists for clerks.

Existing programs are offline, expensive, and provide no measurable impact.

Our Solution

A single platform. Three pillars.

Sanket is an end-to-end accessibility platform — Learn ISL, Interpret conversations, Track compliance — all from a browser.

Learn

Gamified 3-minute daily ISL lessons. Flashcards, quizzes, webcam practice. XP, streaks, badges, leaderboards.

Interpret

Two-way real-time interpreter. Deaf signs → camera detects → clerk sees meaning. Clerk replies → ISL symbols + TTS.

Track

Admin dashboard with real-time analytics. Citizen QR feedback loop. Compliance reports for policymakers.
