The Problem

18 million deaf citizens.
Fewer than 5% clerks know ISL.

Every day, millions of deaf Indians walk into municipal offices and cannot communicate with the clerk across the counter.

18M
Deaf & hard-of-hearing citizens in India
3.5M
Government clerks serving municipal offices
<5%
Have basic ISL proficiency
63M
With hearing/speech disabilities (Census 2011)

Root cause: No accessible, gamified, workplace-integrated ISL training exists for clerks. Existing programs are offline, expensive, and unmeasurable.

Consequence: Deaf citizens rely on written notes, family interpreters, or simply leave without service. No accountability, no recourse.

Our Solution

A single platform. Three integrated pillars.

Sanket is an end-to-end accessibility platform — Learn ISL, Interpret live, Track compliance — from a browser, on existing hardware.

Learn

Gamified 3-minute daily lessons. Flashcards, quizzes, webcam practice. XP, streaks, badges, leaderboards, 25+ municipal signs.

Interpret

Two-way real-time interpreter. Deaf signs → camera detects → clerk sees meaning → clerk replies → ISL symbols + TTS in Hindi/Marathi.

Track

Admin dashboard with real-time analytics. Citizen QR feedback loop. Compliance reports, departmental leaderboards, ISL Champions.
