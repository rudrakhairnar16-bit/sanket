|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| FDMSE-ISL | large ISL video dataset | broad vocabulary research | optional future | verify upstream license before redistribution | do not bundle without verification |

## Critical rule

Never copy an external dataset into the product simply because it is downloadable.

Before integration:
1. verify license;
2. verify source;
3. verify labels;
4. verify consent/usage terms where applicable;
5. preserve attribution;
6. record transformation steps.
